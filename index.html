01Introduction

I didn't start with pixels. I started with people.

My background in research taught me to ask questions, look for patterns, and understand the thinking behind people's decisions.

Over time, I realised that research wasn't the only side of me. I was also deeply drawn to creativity — turning ideas into something people could actually see, interact with, and experience.

UI/UX became the place where those two sides could meet.

Today, I use research to understand the problem, creativity to explore possibilities, and design to turn those possibilities into meaningful products.

04How I think

Before I design the solution, I try to understand the problem.

My design process starts with curiosity.

  1. 1. Observe
  2. 2. Understand
  3. 3. Question
  4. 4. Explore
  5. 5. Design
  6. 6. Refine

I look at:

  1. 01

    People

    What are they trying to achieve? What do they need? What makes the experience difficult?

  2. 02

    Context

    What environment are they using the product in? What constraints influence their decisions?

  3. 03

    Problem

    What is actually causing the friction — and is the obvious problem really the root problem?

  4. 04

    Possibilities

    What different solutions could make the experience simpler, cleaner, or more useful?

  5. 05

    Experience

    How can the final product communicate the solution through structure, interaction, and visual design?

05My journey

A different route into design.

My path into UI/UX wasn't linear — and that's exactly what shaped the way I design.

  1. 01 — Research

    I learned to investigate before I conclude.

    Working with research taught me how to gather information, identify patterns, question assumptions, and look beyond the obvious.

  2. 02 — People

    I learned that there is never just one perspective.

    My experience in teaching helped me understand human behaviour, individual differences, communication, needs, and the importance of adapting how you communicate to the person and situation.

  3. 03 — Creativity

    I discovered that I wanted to build, not just analyse.

    I have always considered myself a creative person. UI/UX gave me a way to combine that creativity with the analytical mindset I had already developed.

  4. 04 — Design

    I found where everything comes together.

    UX gives me the opportunity to understand problems and people. UI gives me the opportunity to turn solutions into visual experiences.

    That combination is what keeps me interested in design.

07Design philosophy

Good design should make the solution feel obvious.

I don't design simply to make an interface look better.
I design to help people understand, decide, navigate, and accomplish something with less friction.
